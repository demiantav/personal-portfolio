LINKEDIN — HERO ATMOSPHERE + MENU LETTER-ROLL
(smoke + lightning + spores)
========================================


----------------------------------------
EN — VERSIÓN PRINCIPAL
----------------------------------------

Just reworked my portfolio hero around two details I can't stop coming back to.

The hero isn't a video. It's a WebGL smoke field: fbm noise with two-step domain warping, lit by random red lightning. Red spores drift in front, and on scroll everything folds into the D of the title. It doesn't need the cursor — it breathes on its own.

The menu is the opposite of loud. Each letter is two stacked copies inside a mask. On hover the top one leaves and a coral one rolls in — one letter at a time, 35ms apart. Barely a movement. Exactly enough.

I keep re-learning the same thing: the best microinteractions are felt, not seen. The moment you notice the technique, I probably overdid it. Restraint is the feature.

The hero pauses off-screen and respects reduced-motion.

Built with GSAP, WebGL and a little canvas maths.
