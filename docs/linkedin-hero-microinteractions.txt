========================================
LINKEDIN — HERO MICROINTERACTIONS
(waves + menu letter-roll)
========================================


----------------------------------------
EN — VERSIÓN PRINCIPAL
----------------------------------------

Just reworked my portfolio hero around two microinteractions I can't stop playing with.

The waves aren't a video. They're ten lines of simplex noise on a canvas — drawn point by point, every frame. Move your cursor and the lines part around it, like water remembering where you've been. Scroll, and they get sliced away, one by one.

The menu is the opposite of loud. Each letter is two stacked copies inside a mask. On hover the top one leaves and a coral one rolls in — one letter at a time, 35ms apart. Barely a movement. Exactly enough.

I keep re-learning the same thing: the best microinteractions are felt, not seen. The moment you notice the technique, I probably overdid it. Restraint is the feature.

Both pause when they leave the viewport and respect reduced-motion.

Built with GSAP and a little canvas maths.

Waves or letters — which one catches your eye first? Link in the comments 👇
